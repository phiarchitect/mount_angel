# Mount Angel Abbey Library: On-Site Architectural Measurement & Observation Guide

**Project**: Mount Angel Abbey Library (St. Benedict, Oregon)  
**Architect**: Alvar Aalto with Vernon DeMars (Executive Architect)  
**Site Visit Date**: September 28, 2026 (1 week from tomorrow)  
**Objective**: Gather critical physical measurements, geometric observations, and reference photographs to calibrate the 3D parametric CAD model.

---

## 1. Executive Summary: What We Know vs. What Needs On-Site Confirmation

Through our constructive geometry analysis of the original architectural drawings, we have established the primary mathematical framework:
- **Radial Fan Structure**: 4 major bays of $23.0^\circ$ each ($92.0^\circ$ total sweep) centered on the circulation desk origin $(0, 0, 0)$.
- **16 Window Spaces**: Exactly 16 equal radial divisions of $5.75^\circ$ each ($9'\text{-}3''$ chord per window, $148'\text{-}0''$ total).
- **Concentric Radii**: Sunken reading well ($R \approx 31'\text{-}10''$), intermediate column ring ($R \approx 62'\text{-}0''$), and outer curtain wall ($R \approx 92'\text{-}5\frac{1}{4}''$).
- **Floor-to-Floor Elevations**: Level 1 to Level 2 is $10'\text{-}0''$; Level 2 to Level 3 is $10'\text{-}0''$; Level 3 to roof eave is $11'\text{-}4''$.

Your physical visit provides the crucial opportunity to calibrate the fine-grain dimensions and verify real-world spatial details.

## 2. Priority 1: Key Calibration Measurements (Pocket Tape / Laser Measure)

These measurements will directly "snap" the parametric formulas to exact reality:

### A. The Core: Circulation Desk & Sunken Reading Well
- [ ] **Circulation Counter**:
  - Distance from the front working edge of the counter to the perimeter curved rail of the sunken well.
  - Height of the curved desk and shelf counter.
- [ ] **Sunken Well Stairs & Terraces**:
  - Count the exact number of steps descending from Level 3 down into the reading well.
  - Measure typical riser height (e.g. $6\frac{1}{2}''$ or $7''$) and tread depth (e.g. $11''$ or $12''$).
  - Measure the width of the stepped terraces where the reading tables sit.

### B. Structural Columns
- [ ] **Interior Round Columns**:
  - Measure the circumference or diameter of one round concrete column (expected $\approx 18''$).
  - Measure the center-to-center distance between two adjacent round columns along the arc.
- [ ] **Perimeter H-Piers (Curtain Wall)**:
  - Measure the width and depth of the brick/masonry H-piers between the window bays (expected $\approx 42''$ deep $\times 28''$ wide).

### C. Finished Vertical Clearances
- [ ] **Level 3 Ceiling**: Finished floor to underside of acoustic wood ceiling slats in the reading room.
- [ ] **Window Sill & Head**: Height of the window sill above Level 3 floor (expected $\approx 2'\text{-}8''$) and window glass height (expected $\approx 6'\text{-}0''$).
- [ ] **Mezzanine Overhang**: Floor-to-soffit clearance under the Level 3 mezzanine deck when standing on Level 2.

## 3. Priority 2: Architectural Details & Observations to Note

### A. The Curved Daylight Monitor Scoop
- [ ] **Daylight Behavior**:
  - Observe how northern daylight enters the curved clerestory monitor above the desk.
  - Note whether the light scoop is lined with white plaster or acoustic slats.
  - Check the profile of the curve (elliptical, parabolic, or cylindrical arc).

### B. Fenestration & Wood Louvers
- [ ] **Vertical Slats**: Note the material, profile, and spacing of the vertical wood shading slats outside the ribbon windows.
- [ ] **Operable Vents**: Note how the lower ventilating wood panels operate beneath the fixed glass.

### C. The Junction between South Wing and Fan
- [ ] Note the physical transition where the rectilinear entrance lobby meets the radial reading room.
- [ ] Note the location of the elevator core and main fire stairs relative to the radial rays.

## 4. Priority 3: Photographic Protocol (Orthographic & Detail Reference)

When taking reference photos, the following angles are particularly valuable for CAD modeling:

1. **Perpendicular Wall Shots (Orthographic Reference)**:
   - Stand square to one full window bay on Level 3 (centered on the bay, camera held horizontal at eye level).
   - Stand square to the South entrance canopy and facade.
2. **The Curved Monitor Scoop**:
   - Shoot directly upward from beneath the circulation counter to capture the curvature of the scoop.
   - Shoot across the reading room from the far North mezzanine looking back toward the scoop.
3. **Sunken Well Cross-Section**:
   - Stand at the bottom of the well looking East or West along the stepped terraces.
4. **Structural Connections**:
   - Close-ups of the column-to-beam junctions.
   - Close-ups of the H-pier to window frame connection.
5. **Entry Portico & Canopy**:
   - Underside of the wood canopy framing and slender support posts.
