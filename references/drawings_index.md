# Mount Angel Abbey Library - Drawings Index

This document serves as an index of the drawings, plans, elevations, sections, and details for the Mount Angel Abbey Library designed by Alvar Aalto.

## Architectural Drawings (A-Series)

### 01-a1_gm-1142x700.jpg 
- **Type**: Site Plan / Title Sheet
- **Title Block**: LOCATION AND ACCESS PLAN / MT. ANGEL ABBEY LIBRARY, ST. BENEDICT, OREGON
- **Contents**: Overall site plan with topography contours, 'List of Drawings' index, 'Key to Symbols', and project title info.

### 02-a2_gm-1140x700.jpg
- **Type**: Site Plan / Sections
- **Title Block**: SITE PLAN
- **Contents**: Detailed site layout including roads, topography, existing buildings, and several sections showing stairs and typical tread details.

### 03-a3_gm-1135x700.jpg
- **Type**: Floor Plan
- **Title Block**: FLOOR PLAN LEVEL #1
- **Contents**: First level floor plan including fan-shaped layout outline, corner details, splitting details, and general notes.

### 04-a4_gm-1144x700.jpg
- **Type**: Floor Plan
- **Title Block**: FLOOR PLAN LEVEL TWO
- **Contents**: Second level floor plan denoting reading areas, stacks, mechanical areas, and general layout notes.

### 05-a5_gm-1145x700.jpg
- **Type**: Floor Plan
- **Title Block**: FLOOR PLAN LEVEL #3
- **Contents**: Main floor plan showing entry, control desk, offices, and radiating curved bookshelves.

### 06-a6_gm-1149x700.jpg
- **Type**: Plan / Details
- **Title Block**: BASIC PLAN LAYOUT
- **Contents**: Geometrical and structural layout of the fan shape, showing radial column lines and several window/jamb details (West Jamb, East Jamb).

### 07-a7_gm-1145x700.jpg
- **Type**: Roof Plan / Details
- **Title Block**: ROOF PLAN & DETAILS
- **Contents**: Layout of the roof with skylights, parapet walls, sill details, flashings, scuppers, and built-up roofing areas.

### 08-a8_gm-1144x700.jpg

- **Title Block**: REFLECTED CEILING PLAN LEVEL #1

### 09-a9_gm-1139x700.jpg

### 10-a10_gm-1145x700.jpg

### 11-a11_gm-1142x700.jpg
